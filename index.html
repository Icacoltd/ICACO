How we work

Root, branch, growth

The same sequence on every engagement, named after the shape the mark is drawn from.

Root

Get clear on what's actually true

We start underground, where the thinking happens before anything is visible: auditing your market position, customer journey and tech stack to name the real gap between where you are and where you want to be.

Branch

Design the direction that fits

Strategy becomes structure, then motion: the channels, systems and decisions that turn a plan into momentum, with Icaco doing the work alongside you where it helps. Every recommendation is built for your business, not lifted from a framework.

Growth

Build for growth that outlasts us

We leave you with something your team can keep running: a plan, the learnings from work done in market, clear owners, and the measures that show it is working.

Ways to work together

Scoped to fit the work

Every engagement is agreed in writing before it starts: what is in scope, what is not, what you receive, and when payment falls due.

Fixed scope

A defined piece of work, for a defined fee

Best for an audit, a go-to-market plan, or an execution programme with a clear start and finish. You know the deliverables, timeline and cost up front, and any third-party budgets are kept separate from our fees.

Retainer

Ongoing support over an agreed term

For teams that want a senior sounding board and hands-on help as they go. A retainer runs for a set term and is reviewed at the end, so it only continues if it is still earning its place.

Performance-linked

Reward tied to the revenue we help generate

For selected sales and partnership work, where Icaco's return is linked to the business it brings in. Suited to companies that want more commercial reach without adding headcount.

Why Icaco

A small root of its own

The name comes from icaco, the Taíno word for the coco plum, a fruit native to Guadeloupe and the wider Caribbean. A reminder that the best growth is grounded in something real.

Icaco was founded by Yoann Chipotel, who has spent more than 12 years in enterprise SaaS revenue across EMEA, in retail, fashion, martech and travel, and twice joined as the founding EMEA sales hire. It is built on one belief: strategy should be practical enough to act on Monday morning.

Questions

Good to know

A few things people usually ask before reaching out.

What

What does Icaco do?

Icaco is a UK consultancy that provides go-to-market strategy and hands-on execution, alongside ecommerce and martech advisory, for growing businesses.

How

Does Icaco only advise, or also help with execution?

Both. Icaco sets the go-to-market strategy and can also run defined execution programmes, such as structured buyer discovery and creator partnership programmes, particularly for brands entering the UK market.

Sectors

Which industries does Icaco work with?

Retail, fashion, martech and travel, plus consumer and design-led brands entering the UK, and other enterprise sectors.

Start

How does an engagement start?

With a short email about your goal, timing and who is involved. If there is a fit, we set up an intro call, then send a written proposal covering scope, deliverables, timeline and fees before any work begins.

Fees

How is Icaco's work priced?

Fixed fees for defined work, retainers for ongoing support over an agreed term, and performance-linked arrangements for selected sales and partnership work. Everything is agreed in writing first, and third-party budgets are kept separate from fees.

Who

Who founded Icaco?

Yoann Chipotel, who has more than 12 years of enterprise SaaS revenue experience across EMEA.

Where

Where is Icaco based?

Icaco is registered in England and Wales, with its registered office in London.
