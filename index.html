Why Icaco

A small root of its own

The name comes from icaco — the Taíno word for the coco plum, a fruit native to Guadeloupe and the wider Caribbean. A reminder that the best growth is grounded in something real.

Icaco Ltd was founded by Yoann Chipotel, drawing on a career in senior enterprise SaaS revenue leadership across EMEA — built on the belief that strategy should be practical enough to act on Monday morning.
